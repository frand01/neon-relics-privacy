Neon Relics – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Neon Relics ("the game") is made by 42001Games. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, relics, scores, settings and lives) is saved only on your device. We do not collect it or send it to our own servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward such as extra lives or moves. Ads are never shown without you choosing to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile (player name and avatar) and your scores are shared with Google Play Games to show global and friends leaderboards. This is handled by Google under its privacy policy and your Play Games settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
